# Retail priorities

Select operating profit/costs and exceptional or adjusting items to explain profit
movements. Include lease liabilities and maturity/commitment notes, related-party
balances and transactions, group guarantees and cash-flow/cash-composition notes.
Cash-equivalent composition can matter when money-market deposits mature.
Employee remuneration needs its own staff-cost note, not just the operating-profit
reconciliation. For undiscounted lease maturities, inspect liquidity risk or
financial risk management tables. A lease accounting policy is insufficient.
Select inventory, receivables and payables notes for working capital when available.
Keep lease balance-sheet amounts separate from undiscounted commitments, adjusted
profit separate from statutory profit, and related-party balances separate from flows.
