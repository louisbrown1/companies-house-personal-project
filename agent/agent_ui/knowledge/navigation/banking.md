# Banking priorities

For CET1 use capital adequacy or regulatory capital notes, and the chief financial
officer or financial review. Published ratios may appear as narrative in front
sections of the report. For LCR and NSFR use
liquidity and funding risk notes; the same note may support both. Cash balances
and customer deposits do not substitute for regulatory liquidity ratios.
For impairments use credit risk and expected credit loss notes. For funding use
deposits, debt securities and subordinated liabilities. Include related parties,
commitments/contingencies and transfers/securitisation where indexed.
Prioritise capital and liquidity, then credit quality and funding, then the
remaining fields. Keep company/group and regulatory reporting perimeters distinct.

Do not treat the dedicated related-party note as complete coverage of related-party
balances. Other-assets and other-receivables notes can describe staff or related-party
loans; other-liabilities notes can describe funding balances. Retrieve indexed
companion notes using the field definitions, then validate the actual relationship,
scope, terms and amounts from the document. Staff loans alone are not group funding.
